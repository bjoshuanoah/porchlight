# Bundled font notice

Porchlight bundles **Roboto Medium** (`Roboto-Medium.ttf`) for the OG
composite (PORCH-079): the overlay typeface renders through bundled glyph
outlines, so compositing is deterministic per hub and independent of the
host's installed fonts.
