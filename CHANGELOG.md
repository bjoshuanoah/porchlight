# porchlight changelog

Released versions of the porchlight package, newest first. Every cut's entry
under its `## v` heading is the line of record the release workflow copies
into that release's notes (the npm-publish workflow fails loudly if the
heading is missing).

## Unreleased

- **[feature]: The engine now answers its own "what did I just see" — locally first, never paid unless you say so (PORCH-133).** Inference tiering arrives as the engine's explicit local ↔ paid routing table: every gated camera event (an arrival, a departure, a loitering) walks its tiers in order — tier 0 renders the free, always-on local facts the detection plane already saw; tier 1 asks the deployment host's own provisioned Ollama (a vision-capable model it can even look at one low-detail snapshot through); tier 2, the paid OpenAI-compatible tier, exists in the table but is never called until you explicitly opt in with your own key. Failures escalate in tier order and every skip is stated, never silent; a description is a "likely reading" with its full provenance (tier, provider, class, model) stored with the event it describes, and video never leaves the hub — not to any provider, ever.

- **[feature]: The Wyze cameras ride Porchlight's own DTLS (PORCH-131).** The engine's vendor-adapter law has its first contract-tested plugin: `wyze://` cameras stream through the engine's stream manager (one upstream connection per camera, every consumer fed, camera drops healing through the manager's backoff ladder) via `@porchlight/wyze` — the reverse-engineered P2P protocol ported in-repo byte-for-byte against the go2rtc reference vectors (the IOTC discovery over UDP 32761 with the Charlie/datagram obfuscation, the DTLS-PSK handshake the camera of record negotiates — ECDHE-PSK/CCAC plus the PSK-CBC fallbacks — frame reassembly with the extended FRAMEINFO trailer where a lost packet resets the frame instead of passing corruption, and the HL K-auth answered under the camera's challenge status classes). The tests run the full session against a fake camera over real UDP, round-trip both DTLS suites against a scripted peer, and prove the one-connection law from inside the stream manager; the live-camera contract suite is env-gated ( PORCHLIGHT_WYZE_LIVE=1 + PORCHLIGHT_WYZE_URL=wyze://…) for the deployment's real fleet. Runs natively on the host only — the DTLS handshake breaks through container UDP paths.

- **[feature]: The admin console arrives — timeline UI v0 with entity events (PORCH-130).** The engine's own console now opens the property's recorded story: a first-run wizard names the deployment and its owner (no password, no external account, nothing else asked — and no vendor-auth surface anywhere), lands the admin directly inside, and is never seen again once done. The calendar timeline browses recorded days and hours per camera, plays the store volume's segments continuously (clips chain into the next recorded one across hours) with real seeking and event marks — every moment the engine flagged (detections and audio alike, from the engine's own event-bus feed) is a mark on the strip, and clicking one seeks straight to it. A recorded-media seam of record (segment index + byte-range segment serving at /segments, /segment, /evidence, /media) lights the hub's homes timeline and seek paths. Around the timeline: a live grid that states plainly when a stream is away, camera setup with RTSP validation before a camera joins, storage and system status planes, and the Porchlight token contract — porch-lamp dark, light, and System default with a pre-paint theme — on a mobile-width bottom tab bar. Zero analytics, zero engagement counts; every absence explains itself in plain language.
- **[feature]: The engine's event bus is provisioned green-field — nothing pre-deployed, ever (PORCH-129).** A fresh deployment walks up with its own bus: the runtime provisions and starts an embedded MQTT broker on loopback before any engine service composes, every service connects at boot, and a restarted service reconnects with no manual step — or the deployment rides its own broker when the config of record points `busUrl` at one, and the setup never provisions a second. The transport decision (MQTT, judged against webhook-only, socket fan-out, and pub/sub daemons) is on record in docs/event-bus-transport.md; the normalized DetectedObject contract now has two deliveries of the same wire payload — the detections topic family on the bus and an HTTP webhook output (fan-out seam keyed off the alertRouting knob) — and every consumer of record subscribes event-driven. A no-Home-Assistant sweep (scripts/no-ha-sweep.mjs) runs with the test matrix on every push so the ruling "Home assistant goes away" stays enforced, not remembered.
- **[feature]: Transcription + searchable audio — the engine hears and remembers what was said (PORCH-155).** The cameras' audio now transcribes as it records: a consumer of the stream manager's own decoded pcm lane (no second camera connection ever exists) turns each camera's speech into transcript records in two regimes — rolling ~1-minute windows that transcribe well under their wall-clock length with a counted, stated cadence budget, and full-span event transcription split into sequential chunks that always carry the FULL span or an explicit partiality state, never a silent truncation. Nothing spoken is ambiguous: speech lands with its text embedded at write time into the local vector store of record (payload refs {cameraId, sourceType, sourceRef, startOffset, endOffset, text}), non-speech windows record an explicit "no speech detected" state instead of an empty field, and the desc-and-transcript law holds — an event page renders description and transcript together or neither. Where someone said something comes back as a search: a spoken phrase ranks across both store families (rolling windows and events) with every result resolved to its timeline path — a rolling window seeks the recorder timeline, an event opens the event page. Which transcriber runs is decided per scope with visible layers (global → recording type → per-camera override, the winning layer always inspectable: whisper-class local binary, OpenAI-spec endpoint, or a local-server endpoint, riding the engine compute posture on the Models/Compute surface), and the retention sweep drops transcript text AND its vectors in the same pass it deletes footage — the index cascade rides the recorder supervisor's sweep, so zero orphan vectors survive a retention cut, count-verified by the orphan check. Transcription is off for every camera until turned on, and a camera with it off runs nothing at all. The admin console gains a Transcripts surface (method scopes + layer inspection, search, explicit-state records, cascade audit), and the status page states the transcription plane's posture plainly — a not-wired plane is unavailable, never silent. (@porchlight/transcript new; config knob transcriptionEnabled off by default; recorder supervisor carries the retention-cascade seam.)
- **[feature]: Stream manager — one upstream connection per camera, every consumer fed (PORCH-154).** The engine has a stream-serving package of its own: @porchlight/stream runs one ffmpeg per camera with a single upstream connection, decodes it into three lanes — rgb frames and audio for in-process consumers, jpeg for the live grid — and copies the full recording stream to per-camera relay ports on localhost, so every consumer rides that one connection and an added viewer adds zero camera load. A slow relay consumer is disconnected by itself (with banded per-socket buffering and an explained log line) instead of backing up the camera, dropped cameras reconnect on their own with the same backoff ladder the recorder uses, and every connect and disconnect states itself on the event bus (porchlight/engine/streams/\<camera\>/up|down). The manager answers control HTTP on the streams port — apply a camera fleet live, read per-camera health, or watch any live camera's MJPEG view directly. The recorder now records camera feeds from those relay lanes instead of opening the camera itself (with a `streams.base` config knob to move the ports; a rebuild of the shifted relay map is part of the same landing), and the config plane registers each camera once for both: the probed frame dimensions become the dims of record in the config source, registration applies the fleet to the recorder and the stream manager, and the engine answers a camera's stream and playback health, its live view through the same manager, and a stream-manager row on the service view — an unreachable manager always says so instead of a blank. The installer runtime composes the stream manager first, recorder second, feeds both into the status page (with the audit sweep's url-law allowlist updated and the live-latency and concurrent-viewer numbers declared benchmark-gated in the package README — none are invented here).
- **Cameras that hear.** Audio event detection arrives as the engine's second detection plane: each camera's audio can be analyzed locally on Apple-class hardware — glass break, dog bark, alarm patterns, raised voices — and the sound facts publish on the same event bus family as visual detections, opening on the timeline with the same clip-and-timestamp evidence path. Audio detection is off for every camera until you turn it on, and a camera with it off runs nothing at all. If the audio analyzer ever stops, only audio events pause — recording and visual detection keep going, and every camera surface states that plainly instead of going quiet.
- **Compute you can see.** The engine's ML stages — detection, event description, audio classification, transcription, Overwatch evaluation, and embedding — now have one Models/Compute surface: each stage runs on Apple's own acceleration by default, can be re-pointed live (native, an OpenAI-spec endpoint, or your own local server) without a restart, and every stage states what it's riding. Any stage forced to CPU, any provider that fails, and even a disabled Local Network permission appear as plain-language states — never a silent slowdown. Setup writes the sane defaults into the config of record, camera URLs are validated through a real probe before a camera joins the fleet, and adding a camera starts recording live. Camera-view sessions never reach the admin surfaces, and the Overwatch composition seams (camera ids, detection stream, composite frames) are documented and contract-tested.
- **Model profiles: the household decides where its agents think.** A new family tab shows which model runs your agent and what class of engine it rides — never an address, key, or token. The hub admin defines profiles and hands out assignments (per agent type or per member), keeps expensive models withheld from members, and sees the explicit "unassigned" state when nothing rides an agent type yet — never a silent default. Parents narrow which of the available profiles their members' agents may use, right inside the family organizer; the hub refuses anything that widens a ruling, in plain language.
- **Teach your porch what to watch for.** Watchlists: name a class (a '63 Chevy Impala), upload example photos, and the hub trains on-device — nothing leaves your home. What it learns joins what the cameras can look for, and a camera alert can subscribe to it like any other thing it sees. Every trained detection shows its confidence, a sure thing says its name, anything less renders "looks like" — and a similar-but-wrong car never borrows the name you taught. Retrain with more photos to sharpen it, and if the model gets it wrong, "Not right" feeds that sighting back into the examples. Deleting a class takes its name, its examples, and its marks with it, and your events keep their plain facts.
- **Faces and plates, recognized at home.** People & Things is the household's own named registry: enroll a face from a photo or a vehicle with its plate, and Porchlight matches it right on the hub — nothing ever goes to a cloud service. Recognized names travel onto your camera events and Overwatch visits ("Mom arrived", "Dad's truck parked"), and anything less than a sure match renders as "looks like" with its confidence shown — never a quiet guess. The household holds the switch: turn recognition off and the registry stays right where you left it, but nothing computes until you turn it back on. Deleting an entry purges its fingerprints — descriptors, plate reads, and every sighting — and one export hands the whole registry back to you.
- **Overwatch: your property's 1,000-foot layer arrives in Porchlight.** A new family tab tracks people, vehicles, and large things across every camera that sees them — one moving thing stays one trail even as it crosses between cameras, and the tracked-objects list shows what's out there right now. Follow a person or a vehicle and its own surface shows where it is and the clips that tell its story in order. Every visit opens on the property map with the cameras that contributed named, arrival through departure on the timeline, and any camera outage renders as an explained gap — never invented footage. You draw each camera's coverage onto your own property map, its zones survive restarts, cameras without a drawn zone simply read as uncovered, and if the association ever joins two visitors that shouldn't be joined, "keep separate" teaches it. The evaluation dial trades depth for battery and compute at a frame rate you set — the range is capped at the measured ceiling and every change lands in the audit trail.
- **Your home's cameras arrive in Porchlight.** The family tab now carries a Cameras surface: a live grid per home with per-feed audio, portrait doorbell feeds at their true shape inline (the full-screen viewer always plays natural aspect), and never a second camera stream opened for an added viewer. Play back a known moment on any recorded camera, browse the activity list, and every absence explains itself in plain language — an offline camera, recording turned off, expired footage, or a camera recording but not detecting never renders as a healthy blank feed.
- **Camera events, told plainly.** Several cameras and frame detections of one routine arrival resolve as ONE event with ONE plain-language alert ("Someone was seen at the Porch."). What detection saw reads as fact; what interpretation concluded only ever reads as a likely reading — when the interpreter cannot answer, the factual label stands and nothing blocks. You can mark an event as not right, and every grant, revocation, and camera change lands in the property audit trail.
- **Camera access is its own authorization.** Home members reach cameras only with a separate camera-access grant from the owner; a visit grant never carries cameras — the bridge refuses in plain language, and camera authorization ends the same way it began: immediately, even from an already-open screen.

## v0.5.3 — 2026-11-26

- **Mix photos and videos in one post.** Pick a few photos, a video, or both, and they ride one post together — each thing shows at its own true shape, videos still act like videos, and everything arrives the way you lined it up.
- **One composer, everywhere.** Writing a post now feels the same on every device: on your phone it opens as a bottom sheet, on your computer as a centered window — but it's the same surface underneath, so staging your pictures, choosing where the post lands, and the moment it publishes all work the same way.
- **When an update is waiting, your porch tells you.** The hub now shows its own banner when an update is ready, and the next time it wakes up it can quietly bring itself up to date — your pages stay fast thanks to the version-matched cache underneath.
- **Your hub can update its own records, safely.** Updates to your porch now carry an ordered, resumable migration step that runs once, keeps going where it left off if anything interrupts it, and never changes records without you starting it.
- **Every post says its thing once.** Posts used to keep their words in two different places; now each post holds one text field, and the old duplicate is retired quietly — the words you wrote are the words that stay.

## v0.5.2 — 2026-10-22

- **Videos play only when you can see them.** A video in the timeline scrolls off your screen and it pauses on its own — no stray sound, nothing playing you can't find. When it scrolls back into view it starts playing again exactly as you left it: quiet if you had it muted, with sound if you turned the sound on.

- **The sound is always the video you're looking at.** If a video has sound, it's the one on your screen — the "where is that sound coming from?" puzzle in the timeline is gone for good.
- **Scrolling just works.** Browsing photos, paging through a post, or scrolling the feed never interrupts itself with a surprise video — playback follows your eyes, not a timer.

## v0.5.1 — 2026-10-22

- **Your porch light is named in your notifications.** Replies, mentions, reactions, and new group moments now arrive on your lock screen with your porch's own name as the main line — never an app name — followed by who sent what: reactions carry the emoji exactly as chosen, replies and mentions carry the sentence that mentions you, and photos arrive without a stray half-written file. This is the first cut the hub composes a notification for, so everything a notification says was written by your own porch.
- **Videos play at their real shape.** Square videos no longer get cropped into a wide frame — every video plays at its true shape, and only very tall phone videos gently fill a tidy portrait box. Open one full-screen and it shows its natural shape from edge to edge.
- **Your mute choice stays yours.** Unmute a video anywhere — in the feed or full-screen — and it stays unmuted: paging away and back, scrolling the timeline, or switching screens never brings the sound down without you saying so.
- **Reactions remember your favorites.** A Frequently Used tab now sits in the emoji picker, keeping the emoji you use most, in order of use, shared across all your devices — and the emoji search finds them all again (💯 included).
- **Saved moments repair themselves.** If a photo fails to load — a slow connection or a momentary blip — the hub quietly tries again moments later and fills the picture in when it arrives. No reload, no error card, no half-finished pictures.
- **No phantom errors.** A "family server is unreachable" message only appears when every attempt has truly been spent — momentary slowness while opening the app just shows the loading screen, and a warning that flashes and vanishes on its own is gone. When the message is earned, it reads the same every time it appears.
- **Your post arrives already lifted.** The moment you publish, your own hand-raise is on it from the first view — and taking a vote back clears it everywhere right away, every click answered by the porch.
- **The porch light arrives with the page.** The dusk boot now shows the porch-light-at-dusk scene: preloads a display-sized still lantern and reuses it across repeat visits without waiting on the app bundle or revalidating the image on every open. The lantern fades in over 300ms when its image loads instead of appearing abruptly; six slow orbiting fireflies glow softly without tails against the quiet navy canvas. Reduced-motion visitors see the still mark immediately after loading.
