# porchlight changelog

Released versions of the porchlight package, newest first. Every cut's entry
under its `## v` heading is the line of record the release workflow copies
into that release's notes (the npm-publish workflow fails loudly if the
heading is missing).

## v0.4.1 — 2026-10-16

- PORCH-093/094: posts and comments have a lifecycle. The author can Edit or Delete their own posts and comments from the card and detail `···` menus; the network owner may also delete anything inside the networks they own, and a group's managing member may delete posts in their group (posts only, attributed to them). Edits are silent — no edited marker, badge, or history, and the content re-renders in place on connected devices. The edit composer opens with the post's content exactly as it stands (never a blank form), carries a Cancel button that abandons the edit, and includes the location control: a post can move between the general timeline and a group on its own network, and delivery follows the new target immediately. Deletes cascade — comments, replies, reactions, votes, and notifications go together; photo and video renditions are removed at delete while the originals hold a 30-day restore window the owner console can reverse (restore re-materializes the post or comment with everything it lost); after 30 days a tombstone hard-purges once and restore is refused.
- PORCH-095: the loading experience is quieter and unmistakably Porchlight — a lantern boot screen climbs navy until the hub is ready, and in-app waits carry reserved-space loader marks instead of jumping layouts, with a spinner sweep on the remaining waits.
- PORCH-096: the compose card always names its room — the location control pre-fills from where the member already is (a group's "+ New post" opens the composer pre-set to that group, and nothing is ambiguous about where a moment will land).

## v0.4.0 — 2026-10-16

Never generated, never summarized.

Served reads carry reactor attribution with origin-containment intact.

- PORCH-084/085: the ruled reply order — comment roots render newest-first with each root's replies chronological right behind its threaded root, on the served reads and the card slices alike (one comparator home, so the rendered order never disagrees with the served order), and the reply composer appears on demand.
- PORCH-086: the link-preview embed facade answers the first tap — the whole compact card is the play act, so one tap on it swaps in the provider player (Apple Music, GitHub, and the rest ride the same surface) without the second-tap dance; GitHub links paint their og:image card on first paint; and the timeline crash the preview facade could drag the page into is fixed.
- PORCH-087: the per-network notification row says what it does — ON means "this network's moments reach you as usual," OFF means "quiet: this network's notifications are paused." The persisted mute flag and hub semantics are untouched.
- PORCH-083: bulk original download — one zip of an album's or a post set's original bytes, served flat from the audited original path on both album and post surfaces.
- PORCH-082: the photo viewer pairs its utilities — Get original and Share live in the viewer's top-right overlay (translucent scrim, tap/hover reveal), and the per-image Get original/Share text utility retires on every media surface.
- PORCH-081: the timeline is live — new moments arrive over the hub's socket feed and the polling cadence is gone.
- PORCH-078/080: share and invite — post detail carries the native share dialog with friendly share text (the copy-fallback outcome reports at the touchpoint), the share act re-enters both `···` card menus through one handler, and the install instructions show the app icon as the outcome.
- PORCH-079: the OG image generator — shared post and invite links unfurl with server-composed, cached images built on the porchlight base art.
- Hub reliability: push subscription registration replaces atomically (the endpoint-keyed upsert converges concurrent registrations on one row), push delivery outcomes land in the hub log on every boot, and VAPID subjects heal to the hub's own DNS host — the form Apple's relay accepts — with the vapid.* boot events logged where hubs keep their logs.

## v0.3.0 — 2026-10-14

- Carries PORCH-071: the npm release route publishes the repository, not a hand-assembled staging tarball (Brian's Oct 14 directive: push the repo to npm). The payload is npm's own packing of the repo checkout (`npm pack -w packages/cli` after the bundling stage pre-stages the runtime closure under the workspace package directory): the registry manifest now carries the repo README and every declared file (the v0.1.1 no-readme listing does not recur), the detached zip-assembly publish route is retired, and the committed package.json version (0.3.0) is authoritative — a loud cut gate fails any run whose tag/version input disagrees with the committed manifests, so tag v-name = package.json version = registry manifest version with no post-registry drift. Route-of-record constraints carried unchanged: OIDC trusted publishing through .github/workflows/npm-publish.yml, zero long-lived NPM_TOKEN, publish before the GitHub release, CHANGELOG gate, the signed SHA256SUMS anchor (registry tarball and release asset are one file), the platform-complete sharp closure and the pruned sub-cap packed size (067 ac-1/ac-3 re-proven on the new tarball).
- PORCH-072: timeline surface trim — the "Family highlights" prominent band, the "Shared moments" heading block (and its supporting line), and the timeline header's create-post, search-moments, and albums buttons are off the surface (the client no longer loads the ranked feed; posting rides compose, search and albums stay on their ruled surfaces); the brand lockup is a single horizontal row, icon left, wordmark right, on every surface.
- PORCH-070: member profile photo from the Profile screen — upload, replace, and clear, with hub-side square crop and compression (no manual crop step; devices never transcode). The face rides the identity, so it renders across every network the member belongs to: timeline and reply headers, the WhoIsHere chooser, owner-console member rows. Renditions are content-addressed squares with long-lived caching and square geometry stamps; replacing retires the prior set whole and clearing reverts to initials, never touching authored content; a PDF or over-5 MB upload refuses in plain language; the member purge cascade retires the set with the account record.
- PORCH-069: timeline cards now keep Lift, Lower, Reply, and React in a compact icon row; Hide and Open conversation live in the header menu. Four latest root comments and their collapsed response counts expand in the feed; mobile posts have a 14px page-background gap instead of a divider.

## v0.2.0 — 2026-10-14

- Carries PORCH-067: the bundled tarball's sharp closure is platform-complete — the platform binaries and their libvips trees for darwin-arm64, darwin-x64, linux-x64 (glibc), linuxmusl-x64, and win32-x64 ride the closure (win32 embeds libvips statically, the others carry the split trees), fetched by exact version at pack time so the tarball never depends on the packer runner's OS. v0.1.1 shipped the packer-runner platform only: non-linux hubs started fine but image media operations failed with a platform error. The first v0.2.0 pack (186.1 MB) failed the npm registry publish cap with 413 Payload Too Large (v0.1.1's known-good registry copy sat at 168.2 MB — the effective PUT ceiling is below 186 MB); the closure is now pruned to the media-necessary set of record: the ten sharp trees (ten of sharp's 26 optional-dep packages; the linux/win32 arches outside the supported set, freebsd, and webcontainers excluded; sharp-wasm32 is sharp's universal fallback, v0.1.1 already carried it) plus zero out-of-support binaries — ffprobe-static's ia32 binary trees (81.2 MB unpacked) pruned from the bundle. Packed tarball: 153.8 MB — 22.6 MB below v0.1.1's 176.4 MB release asset (−14.4 MB vs the 168.2 MB v0.1.1 registry copy) and 32.3 MB below the unpruned first pack; the growth mechanism is only the ten sharp trees, and the ffprobe prune more than pays for them.
- Carries PORCH-064: device notifications opt-in default — absent or freshly created settings rows resolve enabled=false, the send pipeline enforces the master switch first (no send attempt for any event without an explicit enable through the settings route), subscription lifecycle gates on the switch, and no backfill flips members who enabled earlier.
- Carries PORCH-065: address change owner re-auth — a hub moved to a new URL re-binds the owner through the device-link grant: the console's register-the-domain path verifies the new origin before any mutation, re-points every self-reference, and redirects to the self-grant consume route; `porchlight reauth link` / `reauth status` carry the machine-local CLI path.
- Carries PORCH-066: the owner console's hub address panel — one field, one submit; the pre-commit verification refuses an unreachable or foreign origin in plain language before any re-point, redirect auto-consumes the self-grant at the new origin, and the arrival lands the owner bound at the new address with no manual grant handling.
